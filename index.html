What We Do

We specialise in developing business software that simplifies everyday operations and original video games that deliver engaging entertainment experiences. By combining technical expertise with creative thinking, we develop our own software products to address practical business needs and bring imaginative gaming experiences to our audience.

  1. 01

    Research & Discovery

    We identify everyday business challenges, explore opportunities for improvement, and develop an understanding of what makes a software product useful, practical, and effective.

  2. 02

    Design & Planning

    We define each product's objectives, plan its core features, and select the most suitable technologies and programming languages to meet its requirements.

  3. 03

    Development

    We develop our own business software and video games using technologies such as HTML5, CSS, JavaScript, GDevelop, and Python, adapting our technical approach to the needs of each project.

  4. 04

    Testing & Refinement

    We test our products, resolve issues, and refine their functionality to improve reliability, usability, and the overall experience for our users.
